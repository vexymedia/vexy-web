FAQ

Straight Answers.

How many meetings will you book?

We agree on a target of 10–30 qualified meetings before launch, based on your target buyers and calling capacity. If the target isn’t reached in the first 30 days, we keep calling until it is — at no additional campaign fee.

What counts as a qualified meeting?

The right company, a relevant participant, a confirmed business reason to talk and an attended meeting.

Who makes the calls?

Calls are made by human callers prepared and managed for the campaign. VexyLabs oversees the playbook, activity and call quality.

What happens if the prospect doesn't show?

No meeting performance fee applies. A rescheduled meeting only counts after it takes place.

How does pricing work?

A fixed campaign fee covers building and operating the campaign, plus a performance fee for attended, qualified meetings. If we continue calling to reach your agreed target, there is no additional campaign fee; meeting performance fees still apply.
